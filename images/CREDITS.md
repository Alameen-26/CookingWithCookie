# Dish photo credits

The dish photos are openly licensed stock photos from Wikimedia Commons. They are
placeholders until Cooking with Cookie has its own photos, so the food shown is not
Cookie's own cooking.

Changes made to every photo: cropped to 4:3, resized to at most 800 px wide, and
saved as a JPEG embedded in an `.svg` file. (The publishing tool used for this
repository can only upload text files, so each photo is wrapped in a small SVG;
browsers show it like a normal image.) Adapted versions of CC BY-SA photos are
shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.
